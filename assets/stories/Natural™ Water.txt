Cold opening:
Front view of a worker dashing through the forest with a leaf containing water. 

Or

Text: this is not an actual commercial, it is a skit.
Cut to: 

Gorgeous cinematic stream.
"This is the water you drink every day."
"Pure. Natural."
Cut to a bottling plant.
"But modern companies have forgotten what 'natural' really means."

1. Leaves
Workers carefully scoop water into giant leaves.
It takes forever.
One trips and loses a whole day's work.
"At first, we transported every drop using only materials found in nature."
Pause.
"But then we realized..."
"...the leaves weren't naturally growing there."
2. Human Chain
Dozens of employees stand a few feet apart from each other, going from the river to the factory.
Each person fills their mouth, spits into the next person's mouth, and so on.
The narrator delivers it with complete seriousness.
"So we created the world's first all-natural hydration transfer network."
A worker accidentally misses.
The entire line groans.
Pause.
"But then we realized..."
"...mouths weren't naturally growing there."

2. Reroute 
Workers in hard hats survey the land. They string up measuring tape, plant little flags, and dig a narrow channel toward the factory with complete seriousness.
Water trickles a few inches.
One worker gives a thumbs up.
Narrator:
"We briefly considered rerouting the river."
Beat.
"But then we realized..."
Pause.
"...the new path wasn't naturally there."
Everyone silently fills the trench back in.

3. Worker Bees
Tiny harnesses.
Miniature buckets.
A supervisor with a clipboard watches bees carry microscopic droplets.
Time-lapse showing hours of work.
They've collected maybe half a teaspoon.
"Finally... we hired nature's own workforce."
Then a bee flies away.
The supervisor yells, "There goes Steve!"

Pause.
"But then we realized..."
"...the bees weren't naturally domesticated."

"We briefly considered relocating nature."
Beat.
"...but then we realized..."
"...the river wasn't naturally over there.”

Ending
"But after years of research..."
Cut back to the original stream.
Someone kneels down and takes a sip.
"We realized..."
"...the most natural water..."
Beat.
"...is the water you don't move."
