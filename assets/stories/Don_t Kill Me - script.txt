Title: Don't Kill Me

Genre: Thriller / Dark Comedy
Location: Suburban home, night


---

INT. LIVING ROOM – NIGHT

Dimly lit. A quiet suburban house. The TICK of a clock breaks the silence.

A window CREAKS OPEN. A GLOVE grips the sill. A SHADOW slinks inside.

ROBBER (early 30s, jittery, in dark clothes)
Creeps through the room, flashlight flickering. He freezes as a floorboard CREAKS upstairs.

CUT TO:

INT. BEDROOM – NIGHT

Lights flick on. The bedroom door opens slowly. A figure steps into the hallway.

HOUSE OWNER (JACK) (mid-40s, in pajamas)
Spotting the robber downstairs. Pale. Eyes wide.

JACK
(terrified whisper)
Please… please don’t kill me…

ROBBER
(startled, raising the gun slightly)
What? Speak up!

Jack’s face changes. He clenches his fists, trembling — with something deeper than fear.

JACK (screaming)
DON’T KILL ME!!

He charges.

ROBBER
(panicking, backs away)
Whoa—! No no—!

He drops the gun with a CLATTER. Turns to run.

JACK
(yelling)
YOU PICKED THE WRONG HOUSE!

Jack tackles him from behind. They crash to the floor.

INT. LIVING ROOM – MOMENTS LATER

Chaotic sound: fists, screams, a lamp smashing. Blood spatters the beige carpet.

ROBBER
(choking)
Please… don’t kill—!

THUD. THUD. Silence.

JACK (panting, over the body)
I said don’t kill me.

He stands up slowly, staring at his bloody hands. Sirens in the distance.

SMASH TO BLACK.

TEXT ON SCREEN:
Some fears should be mutual.
