OFFICE WILDLIFE DOCUMENTARY
FADE IN:
INT. OFFICE – DAY
Soft nature documentary music plays.
A quiet office. Employees sit at desks under fluorescent lights.
One EMPLOYEE casually eats potato chips while scrolling, facing away from the desks. 
An EMPLOYEE stares blankly at a computer screen, motionless.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
(in the style of a prestigious nature documentary)
Here we see the office worker... in its natural habitat.
The employee continues staring into the void.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
Moments ago, it was doing absolutely nothing...
A MANAGER suddenly enters the office.
Instantly, the employee springs to life, typing furiously.
The chip-eating employee slowly lowers the bag beneath the desk without breaking eye contact with the manager - sweat visibly dripping down his face. 


NARRATOR (V.O.)
...but a manager has entered the habitat.
CLOSE ON the employee aggressively pounding the keyboard.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
Notice the defensive behavior.


BEAT 2
MACRO SHOT – COMPUTER SCREEN
The screen fills with complete nonsense:
"asdfasdfasdfasdfasdf"
NARRATOR (V.O.)
The worker initiates a ritual known as aggressive typing.
The employee nods seriously while continuing to mash keys.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
This creates the illusion of productivity.


BEAT 3
The employee dramatically opens an enormous spreadsheet.
Rows and columns stretch endlessly across the screen.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
Next, the worker performs the spreadsheet display.
The employee scrolls with exaggerated focus.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
No one knows what the spreadsheet does...
A pause.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
Not even the worker.


BEAT 4
Across the office, ANOTHER EMPLOYEE suddenly grabs a laptop and power-walks down the hallway.
The documentary camera WHIP PANS to follow.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
In rare cases, the worker may attempt a migration.
The employee marches with intense purpose despite clearly having nowhere specific to go.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
This signals importance to nearby managers.


BEAT 5
The manager finally leaves.
Instantly, the original employee stops typing and resumes staring blankly into space.
Silence.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
(quietly)
And just like that...
A beat.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
The threat has passed.
Suddenly—
The manager's head quickly swings around the doorway.
An ominous musical sting BLARES.
[OMINOUS MUSIC]
The employee jolts upright.
MANAGER
Ah, Bob. I see you're working diligently as always.
The manager's eyes drift across the office.
He spots an empty desk.
MANAGER
Oh, what's this? Jared is in the bathroom?
He notices Jared's computer is still unlocked.
MANAGER
And his computer's still on?
A sinister smile spreads across his face.
MANAGER
Well... I might just take a quick little peek.
He power-walks toward the desk.
WIDE SHOT
JARED enters from another doorway, staring at his phone.
He looks up.
Sees the manager approaching his computer.
SPLIT SCREEN SHOT
They lock eyes.
A beat.
CLOSE ON – JARED
Jared immediately quickens his pace.
CLOSE ON – MANAGER
The manager sees Jared moving.
He walks faster.
CLOSE ON – JARED
Jared breaks into a light jog.
CLOSE ON – MANAGER
The manager is now speed-walking at maximum velocity.
CLOSE ON – JARED
Full jog.
CLOSE ON – MANAGER
Now sprinting.
CLOSE ON – JARED
Also sprinting.
WIDE SHOT
Both men charge across the office.
Coworkers scatter.
Slow motion.
They DIVE simultaneously toward the computer.
The chip employee takes a bite of a chip. 


Soft inspirational music swells.


Their eyes meet.
The manager's expression softens.
MANAGER (breathy)
Jared...
Their arms reach toward each other.
For a brief, beautiful moment, it seems they may embrace.
The manager suddenly SHOVES off Jared's shoulders, flying over Jared and propelling himself toward the keyboard.
The music RECORD SCRATCHES.


The manager stretches desperately toward the keyboard.
His finger reaches—
CLICK.
The WINDOWS KEY.
The Sudoku game appears.
The manager freezes.
Tears begin forming in his eyes.
MANAGER
Yes!
He chokes back emotion.
MANAGER
You were playing Sudoku.
He wipes away a tear.
MANAGER
I'm sorry... this is just so emotional.
Coworkers stare in stunned silence.
MANAGER
This...
A sniff.
MANAGER
This is the first time I have actually...
He struggles to continue.
MANAGER
Actually done something.
He composes himself.
MANAGER
I'm going to go take the rest of the day off.
The manager exits.
He stops in the doorway and turns back.
MANAGER
Oh, yeah.
A beat.
MANAGER
Jared, you're fired.
The manager leaves.
Silence.
Jared remains on the floor.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
The office ecosystem can be a cruel and unforgiving place.
10 seconds go by. 
The chip guy offers him a chip.
FADE OUT.
