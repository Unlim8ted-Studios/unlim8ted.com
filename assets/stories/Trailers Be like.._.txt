Summary:
"Trailers Be Like" is a comedic parody trailer for a murder mystery film that hilariously spoofs the way modern trailers often reveal too much. Packed with dramatic narration, overly intense music, and a flood of plot points that seem to spoil the entire movie, the trailer leans into the absurd — turning the mystery into a joke by showing nearly every twist, character, and "shocking reveal" upfront. With scattered gags and self-aware humor throughout, it playfully mocks trailer tropes while pretending to be the most spoiler-filled murder mystery preview ever made.

[INT. MANSION – NIGHT] [A body hits the ground. Gasps echo. Lightning flashes.] V.O.“...Something finally did.”
