I (Unwillfully) Let My Cat Answer My Emails for ~1 week

Premise: Your cat walks across your keyboard during one email… so you commit to letting it respond to everything for a week.
Day 1:
Out-of-office auto reply written by cat:
“;;;;;;;;;mmmmMMMMMMmow”
Client replies:
“Is this a metaphor?”
Day 2 – Escalation
Cat accidentally hits “Reply All.”
Sends: asdfjjjjjjjjjjjjjjjjjj
Boss replies: “Bold strategy.”
Day 3 – Unexpected Success
One client interprets it as minimalist branding.
“We love the abstract tone.”
Mid-Week Twist:
Your cat types one perfectly normal sentence by random chance.
“Let’s circle back next week.”
Everyone thinks you’ve “grown.”
Climax:
You regain control of your inbox.
You try writing a normal email.
It sounds worse than the cat’s.
Button Joke:
Cut to cat sitting on laptop.
Lower-third text:
“Senior Communications Director.”
