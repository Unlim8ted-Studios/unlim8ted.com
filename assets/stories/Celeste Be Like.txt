Celeste Be Like

INT. APARTMENT – DAY

A completely normal living room.

ALEX stands at one end.

On the coffee table:

The TV remote.

Too far to comfortably reach without walking around the couch.

ALEX
(quietly)
Okay.

Beat.

ALEX takes one step forward.

A tiny dash sound plays.

No one acknowledges it.

ALEX freezes.

ALEX
…You heard that, right?

ROOMMATE (O.S.)
Heard what?

ALEX looks down.

A faint blue trail lingers behind their foot.
Gone instantly.

ALEX
Oh no.

The room is still.

ALEX slowly looks at the coffee table.

The remote has rotated.
Just slightly.

ROOMMATE (O.S.)
You’re not doing this again.

ALEX
It moved.

ROOMMATE walks in, sees nothing unusual.

ROOMMATE
It didn’t.

ALEX stares.

The remote slides forward an inch.

Both of them see it.

Silence.

ROOMMATE
…Okay.

A tiny dash sound.
Very faint.
Like it came from inside a shoebox.

ALEX crouches slowly and peers over the edge of the table.

Nothing there.

Another tiny dash.

The HDMI cable twitches.

ROOMMATE
Is that Bluetooth.

ALEX
That’s not how Bluetooth works.

The TV turns on by itself.

Blue screen.

“NO SIGNAL.”

The remote inches forward again.

Like something is repeatedly bumping it.

Tiny impact sounds.

thk.
thk.
thkthkthk.

ROOMMATE
…Is there a rat?

A red blur streaks across the table.

Both of them flinch.

Suddenly—

MADELINE is standing on the remote.

Idle animation.
Breathing.
Completely serious.

ROOMMATE
Why is she two feet tall.

ALEX
Perspective.

MADELINE looks at the HDMI cable.
It’s slightly unplugged.

She looks at it.
Then at the couch arm.
Then at the wall.

Planning.

ROOMMATE
She’s not actually going to—

Dash.

She launches at the cable.

Misses.

Death sound.

She vanishes.

ROOMMATE
Oh my god.

Respawn sound.

She reappears on the bookshelf.
Still determined.

ROOMMATE
Is she dying??

ALEX
It’s fine. It doesn’t count.

Dash.

She hits the HDMI cable again.

It moves half a millimeter.

ROOMMATE
This is the worst way to fix electronics.

Dash.

Miss.

Death.

Respawn.

Dash.

Cable moves another millimeter.

ROOMMATE
Just push it in with your hand.

ALEX
No, she’s lining it up.

ROOMMATE
She has hands.

Dash.

Direct hit.

The HDMI cable snaps perfectly into place.

The TV instantly displays Netflix.

Silence.

MADELINE stands triumphantly on the remote.

Chapter Clear jingle plays faintly.

On-screen (imaginary):

CLEAR TIME: 02:47
DEATHS: 18
CABLE ALIGNED

ROOMMATE
…We could have just—

MADELINE slowly turns.

Looks at ROOMMATE.

Deadpan.

Dash.

She dashes directly into ROOMMATE’s shin.

ROOMMATE
OW— what—

Respawn sound.

She’s now standing on ALEX’s shoulder.

ALEX
She only dashes forward.

ROOMMATE
Why is she solving household problems like this.

MADELINE looks at the light switch across the room.

Beat.

ALEX
Don’t.

MADELINE crouches slightly.

ROOMMATE
Don’t encourage her.

Dash sound.

Lights turn off.

Darkness.

From the dark:

thud.

Death sound.

Respawn sound.

MADELINE reappears glowing faintly in idle animation.

ROOMMATE
She died turning off the lights.

ALEX
Bad cycle.

MADELINE dashes again.

Lights turn back on.

She stands proudly on the switch plate.

ROOMMATE
…Okay that one was efficient.

MADELINE nods once.

Then looks at the thermostat.

Both of them freeze.

ALEX
Absolutely not.

Cut to black.

Tiny dash sound.

ROOMMATE (O.S.)
WHY IS IT 58 DEGREES.
