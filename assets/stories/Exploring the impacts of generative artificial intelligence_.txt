Early rule based chatbots

ACT II – REACTION
CUT TO: ON SCREEN TEXT
ENERGY
CAMERA FLYTHROUGH TO: EXT. POWER GRID - DAY
Transmission lines stretching across land.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“Running large AI models requires enormous computing power.
Most of that power comes from data centers.”
CUT TO: DATA CENTER - DAY
Cooling systems, fans, servers.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“As AI use has increased, global data center energy demand has risen sharply.”
ON SCREEN TEXT WITH RISE IN DIAGRAM
Global data center electricity use has more than doubled since 2010
(MIT, Ding et al.)
Expert 2 (to camera)
“This has raised concerns about carbon emissions– especially in regions that still rely on fossil fuels.”
CUT TO: POSITIVE COUNTERPOINT
INT. CONTROL ROOM - DAY (STOCK FOOTAGE)Energy dashboards, graphs updating.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“At the same time, AI has been used to reduce emissions by optimizing energy grids, improving efficiency, and supporting renewable energy.”
Expert 1
“So the technology that increases energy demand can also help manage it.”

WATER
Cinematic synth drone fades in.
CAMERA FLYTHROUGH TO: EXT. DRY LANDSCAPE - DAY
Slow pan.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“Energy is not the only environmental cost.”
Fade in new music.
CUT TO: DATA CENTER COOLING
Water vapor rises.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“Many data centers rely on water-based cooling systems.”
ON SCREEN TEXT WITH SLIDE IN DIAGRAM
Projected AI water use by 2030 is equivalent to millions of people. (UNEP)
Expert 2 (to camera)
“That has raised concerns in water-stressed regions.”
CUT TO: MODERN DATA CENTER FOOTAGE
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“However, newer facilities are experimenting with closed loop cooling, air cooling and water recycling– which could reduce overall impact in the future, although we still need to make more steps towards this."

COMMUNITIES
Cinematic synth drone fades in.
CAMERA FLYTHROUGH TO: EXT. SMALL TOWN - DAY
Homes near industrial areas.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“The effects of AI infrastructure are often felt more strongly at the local level.”
New music fades in.
CUT TO: FOOTAGE OF TOWNS WITH DATA CENTERS
Slow panning shots.
NARRATOR (V.O.)
“There are economic benefits — jobs, although very few, and tax revenue, but also concerns about infrastructure strain and property value loss.
Expert 2 (TO CAMERA -> V.O.)
“One danger that is imperative to consider is the health effects which AI Data centers can cause. In one case, the Professor of biostatistics at Harvard’s School of Public Health had a team investigate a town which was planning to create a power plant to power a new AI Data Center. While the developers of the plant stated to the community that there would be minimal impacts, once the professor investigated the situation his team found that ‘the plant’s emissions would expose 1.2 million people to increased health risks from air pollution and lead to $625 million in additional healthcare costs.’”
CUT TO: COMMUNITY INTERVIEW
Interview footage
NARRATOR (V.O.)
