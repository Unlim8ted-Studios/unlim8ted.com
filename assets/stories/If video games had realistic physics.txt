IF VIDEO GAMES HAD REALISTIC PHYSICS

----------------------------------------
OPENING
----------------------------------------

INT. FANTASY ITEM SHOP – DAY

A merchant finishes packing an enormous adventurer's backpack.

MERCHANT
You'll need these to defeat the dragon.

He slides the backpack across the counter.

The hero confidently grabs the straps.

He braces himself...

...and barely manages to lift it.

MERCHANT
...You're going to want to make two trips.

The hero forces a smile, throws the backpack onto his shoulders, and stumbles toward the door.

HERO (O.S.)
No... I've got it...

TITLE CARD:

IF VIDEO GAMES HAD REALISTIC PHYSICS

----------------------------------------
ACT 2 – LEAVING TOWN
----------------------------------------

The hero slowly drags the backpack through town, exhausted.

Deep grooves trail behind it in the dirt.

A town guard points ahead.

GUARD
The dragon's just over that hill.

The "hill" is nothing more than a chest-high rock ledge.

The hero sighs and drops the backpack.

After several failed attempts, he finally manages to climb onto the ledge.

Relieved, he reaches down for the backpack...

...but it's just out of reach.

He stretches farther.

Still can't reach it.

He sighs.

Climbs all the way back down.

----------------------------------------
ACT 3 – THE JOURNEY
----------------------------------------

Later...

The hero crests another hill, dragging the backpack behind him with a rope.

A river blocks the path.

A tiny wooden rowboat sits at the shore.

The hero smiles.

He places the backpack into the boat.

The boat instantly flips upside down.

Beat.

The hero just stands there, staring.

HERO
...Ugh.

----------------------------------------
ACT 4 – RANDOM ENCOUNTER
----------------------------------------

The hero trudges through a forest, backpack soaked.

A wolf steps onto the path.

Epic battle music swells.

The hero dramatically reaches into the backpack for his sword.

He pulls out...

An apple.

He throws it aside.

He digs again.

A frying pan.

Another apple.

A rope.

A cabbage.

Finally—

His sword.

He triumphantly looks up.

The wolf is gone.

Only its tail disappears into the trees.

The hero sighs.

----------------------------------------
ACT 5 – THE DRAGON
----------------------------------------

Sunset.

The dragon stands atop a rocky cliff.

Huge.

Terrifying.

The hero drops the backpack.

He cracks his neck.

Rolls his shoulders.

Charges.

He attempts a heroic combat roll.

Halfway through—

AAAGH!

He grabs his hamstring and collapses.

The dragon watches in silence.

Finally...

DRAGON
...Do you need a minute?

----------------------------------------
ENDING
----------------------------------------

The dragon helps the hero to his feet.

It effortlessly picks up the backpack.

Together, they walk back into town.

The merchant sees them.

MERCHANT
Oh, good... you're back.

He disappears behind the counter.

A moment later, he returns pushing a cart stacked high with arrows.

MERCHANT
You forgot these.

The hero stares directly into the camera.

CUT TO BLACK.
