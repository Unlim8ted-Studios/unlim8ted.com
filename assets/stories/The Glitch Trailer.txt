TRAILER SCRIPT: THE GLITCH
FADE IN:
BLACK SCREEN.A distant, distorted hum. The sound of static. A heartbeat.
TEXT ON SCREEN:What if reality... wasn’t real?
QUICK FLASHES:
A cozy bedroom. An alarm clock ticks—too fast, too slow.
A bucket slipping into a rushing stream.
A shadow flickering in the woods—watching.
A pair of eyes glowing from the dark.
VOICEOVER (FATHER, WHISPERED, ECHOING):"Come closer… touch the rune…"
CUT TO BLACK.
A glitch flickers on the screen. Something is… wrong.
QUICK MONTAGE:
A vent cover bursting open.
A terminal flashing lines of unknown code.
A meatball with googly eyes rolling behind a tree.
A city skyline, but the buildings… distort.
A family running as the world collapses behind them.
A door opening… but to where?
TEXT ON SCREEN:Some experiments should never be reset.
SCIENTIST 1 (V.O.)"They weren’t supposed to evolve this far."
SCIENTIST 2 (V.O.)"It doesn’t matter. Thirteen hours until the next reset."
MAIN CHARACTER (V.O.)(frantic, breathless)"I think… I think I’m real. Aren’t I?"
CUT TO BLACK.Silence. Then—
A CLICK. A lightbulb flickers on. A plan. A choice.
TEXT ON SCREEN:From the creators of bizarre adventures and the life you never knew a meatball could live…
GLITCH EFFECT.
TEXT REFORMS:UNLIM8TED STUDIOS PRESENTS…
A final glimpse—
A desperate leap toward a closing portal.
A hand reaching for a glowing console.
A single tear, frozen in time.
CUT TO BLACK.
A digital chime. A system reboot.
TEXT ON SCREEN:"The Glitch"
“Coming this summer”
A SINGLE BEEP.
TEXT GLITCHES OUT.
FADE TO BLACK.
